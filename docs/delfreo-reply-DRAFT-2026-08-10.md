Dear Dr. Del Freo,

Thank you very much — the four PDFs arrived safely, and with Olivier's 1996–2000 report you
have also answered my bibliographic question (Pasiphae III (2009), 187–197; I had not
managed to trace it). I am very grateful; the files will of course not circulate further.

As promised, please find attached the results of the indexing: a machine-readable index of
the Linear A entries across the five rapport generations 1996–2021 — 125 entries, each with
designation, find data, the readings as reported in the respective rapport, the operative
citation with page numbers, and the item's reporting history across generations (announced,
published, revised, renumbered, or withdrawn, e.g. PH Wb 53). A README documents the
conventions. The index is CC BY, so please use it in any way that is helpful; I would of
course be glad if it proved useful as a companion to the series.

Since the index is cross-referenced against the public digital Linear A corpora, a few
observations surfaced that may interest you (details and the full list in the README):
the lineara.xyz-derived dataset follows your Khania numbering (KH Zb 98, 99, 100) but the
editors' numbering for the Akrotiri lamp (Zb 15), and mixes ad-hoc labels for the Delta 18a
tablets — of which one can be identified mechanically with THE 7 (its numerals 7 and 46
match Boulotis's readings) and another with THE 10, while the TELA+SE fragment THE 8 is
absent from that dataset (SigLA does carry it). That dataset also retains a few readings your series has since superseded
(the numeral on KN Zb 27; AB 54 on KH Wc 2124) and misassigns two site labels (the Arkhanes
series appears under "Arkalochori", LACH Za 1 under "Larani"). None of this is a criticism
of anyone — it is exactly the kind of drift a structured index makes visible, and I have
documented it on my side.

If you spot errors in the index — I am sure there are some — I would be very glad to hear
of them and will correct and re-send.
