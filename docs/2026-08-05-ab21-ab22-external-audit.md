# 2026-08-05 — External claim CONFIRMED: AB21/AB22 (OVIS/CAP) inversion in the lineara.xyz lineage

**Status: CONFIRMED (external data-defect claim, re-adjudicated from in-repo witnesses).
Impact on published results: NONE (see §4). Silver-corpus data defect: REAL — candidate
data ERRATUM; remediation deferred to an explicit decision (§5).**

Articles triggered: Art. XI (source dependency — silver derives from lineara.xyz),
Art. XVII (append-only record — this note, no silent fix), invariant 12 (all counts below
generated by `scripts/audit_ab21_ab22.py`).

## 1. The external claim

Tsirkas, C. (2026), *Auditing the Unread — A Corpus Validation Framework for Undeciphered
Scripts, with a Demonstration on Linear A*, defect **D4** (§4.2), AI-assisted research
artifact: <https://github.com/ChristosTsirkas/corpus-validation-for-undeciphered-scripts-linear-a>

Claim: the Douros tabulation behind **lineara.xyz** — the same public digital edition our
silver corpus is normalised from (`scripts/corpus_io.py`) — systematically swaps the
livestock logograms **AB21 (\*21, OVIS, sheep)** and **AB22 (\*22, CAP, goat)** at 18
occurrence-sites across 14 documents (HT/KH/KN/PH/ZA). Younger's records and the decoded
SigLA database agree against it at all 15 SigLA-attestable sites.

The claim was found via the author's public release of 2026-08-05 (independent,
Claude-assisted project; it does not derive from logos — different corpus pipeline,
different SigLA decoder implementation, none of logos's machinery or results appear in it).

## 2. Re-adjudication from in-repo witnesses (no trust in the external artifact)

Witness 1 — **SigLA decode** (`corpus/bronze/sigla_browse_2026/sigla_documents.json`, from
`scripts/sigla_decode.py`; family level only, SigLA carries no M/F variants).
Witness 2 — **Younger's own records** (`corpus/bronze/younger_lineara/HTtexts.txt`,
`misctexts.txt`), checked by eye for every flagged document (OVIS/CAP wordings).

Run: `python3 scripts/audit_ab21_ab22.py` (per-token table). Result over all 35 silver
documents carrying \*21/\*22-family tokens (49 tokens total):

| verdict vs SigLA | documents | tokens |
|---|---|---|
| AGREE | 21 | 21 |
| **INVERTED** (every token flipped) | **11** | **15** |
| MIXED (PH 31a, PH 31b — 1 divergent token each) | 2 | 12 |
| NOT_IN_SIGLA (HT Wc 3024) | 1 | 1 |

Inverted documents: HT20, HT64, HT132, HT136a, KH6, KN28a, KNWc29, ZA9, ZA22, ZA26a,
ZA26b — matching Tsirkas's D4 list exactly, 15/15 attestable sites, both directions.
Younger agrees with SigLA at every site checked (e.g. HT 132 `OVIS f` where silver has
`*22F`; HT 20 `CAP m` where silver has `*21M`; ZA 22 `OVISf/OVISm` ×4 where silver has
`*22F/*22M`). Two independent witnesses, zero exceptions: this is a systematic
mis-mapping in the tabulation, not misreadings.

Additional defect found while testing (not in D4 as an omission): **HT38** — silver
carries *no* \*21/\*22-family token at all, while SigLA reads `AB21` and Younger reads
`OVIS 3` (line .2). The lineara.xyz lineage reads \*22 there per Tsirkas; our
normalisation apparently dropped the token. Separate, unadjudicated normalisation defect.

Nuances vs the external paper: (a) our SigLA decode **does** contain PH 31a/31b, which
Tsirkas's register marks "not in SigLA" — at family level PH 31b agrees with silver at
7/8 positions, PH 31a at 3/4, i.e. the PH 31 divergences are instance-level, not a
blanket inversion; (b) the inversion is therefore **not** a corpus-wide relabel — 21
documents carry correct readings — so it cannot be waved off as a harmless bijection.

- `*21M/*21F/*22M/*22F` are classified **LOGO** in `corpus/silver/signs_ontology.json`;
  the syllabogram inventories (conservative + exploratory) contain **zero** such tokens.
  The paper's probes (morphology, segmentation, phonology, cross-script) run on
  syllabogram sequences and never see these signs.
- `paper/tacl/body.tex`: zero mentions of \*21/\*22/OVIS/CAP/sheep/goat. The frozen
  paper and the Zenodo v1.0 preprint are untouched; §(f) hashes stand.
- Analysis scripts: no analysis addresses these sign identities (grep hits are
  `CONF_CAP_MODEL` / `ACCEPTED` false positives).
- Where it COULD matter going forward: anything that attaches commodity semantics
  (sheep vs goat) to documents in the D4 set — e.g. the observable-admin-channel
  programme's commodity-logogram work (branch `research/observable-admin-channel-recovery`)
  should be checked before any *new* claim touches HT20/HT64/HT132/HT136a/KH6/KN28a/
  KNWc29/ZA9/ZA22/ZA26a/ZA26b/PH31/HT38.

## 5. Remediation options (decision deferred, Art. XVII — no silent rebuild)

1. **Correction layer in `corpus_io.py`** keyed to the 15 token-sites (+ HT38 restore),
   emitting a new, versioned silver — SUPERSEDING data version, old silver retained for
   reproducibility of past artifacts.
2. **Divergence register only** (Tsirkas's own policy): record, don't rewrite; require
   any future analysis touching these docs to run under both readings.
3. Do nothing until a new analysis actually touches the affected tokens.

Option 2 is the conservative default consistent with the append-only record; option 1 is
warranted the moment any commodity-level analysis resumes.
